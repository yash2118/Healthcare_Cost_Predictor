# Healthcare Cost Predictor — AI-Powered Healthcare Cost & Risk Analysis
## Project Overview
Healthcare Cost Predictor is an AI-powered healthcare analytics platform designed to estimate individual healthcare expenses, classify patients into risk tiers, and provide data-driven healthcare insights. The platform helps:
- Insurers optimize premium pricing
- Healthcare providers identify high-risk patients
- Patients better understand potential medical expenses
## Why This Project?
- Healthcare costs are influenced by multiple factors such as: age, BMI, smoking habits, chronic conditions, and lifestyle patterns.
- Insurance companies often struggle with accurate premium pricing, while patients face unexpected medical expenses.
- This project was built to provide transparent healthcare cost estimation, risk analysis, and AI-driven healthcare insights.
## Problem Statement
Traditional healthcare cost estimation methods lack transparency and accurate risk assessment. This project solves that problem by creating:
- AI-based healthcare cost prediction system
- Risk tier classification
- Premium optimization tools
for smarter healthcare and insurance decision-making.
## Key Features
### AI-Powered Cost Prediction
- Predicts individual healthcare expenses using patient and lifestyle data
### Risk Tier Classification
- Categorizes patients into Low, Medium, and High risk groups
### Explainable AI (XAI)
- Uses SHAP visualizations to explain prediction-driving factors
### Smoking Cessation Analysis
- Shows healthcare cost reduction after quitting smoking
### Premium Optimization
- Helps insurers optimize premium pricing and portfolio risk
## Workflow
Patient Data -> Data Preprocessing -> Machine Learning Prediction -> Risk Classification -> SHAP Explainability -> Cost Insights & Premium Analysis
## Key Insights
- Smoking significantly increases healthcare costs.
- Chronic conditions and hospital visits strongly impact medical expenses.
- Lifestyle habits influence patient risk levels.
- Explainable AI improves transparency in healthcare predictions.
